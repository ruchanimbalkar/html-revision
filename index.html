What is HTML ?

HTML (HyperText Markup Language) is the most basic building block of the Web. It defines the meaning and structure of web content. Other technologies besides HTML are generally used to describe a web page's appearance/presentation (CSS) or functionality/behavior (JavaScript). Courtsey: mdn

Inline Vs. Block Elements

Most elements are either block- or inline- level elements.

Block-level elements begin on a new line, stacking on top of the other, and occupy any available width. Block-level elements may be nested inside one another and may nest inline-level elements.

Inline-level elements do not begin on a new line. They fall into the normal flow of a document, lining up one after the other, and only maintain the width of their content. Inline level elements may be nested inside one another; however they cannot nest block-level elements.
